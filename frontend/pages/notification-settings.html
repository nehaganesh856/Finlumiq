Set up on Android

Use Chrome on your phone for the simplest installation flow.

1
Open Finlumiq over HTTPS

Open your deployed Finlumiq website in Chrome on your Android phone.

2
Enable notifications

Tap “Enable notifications & send test” and choose Allow when Chrome asks.

3
Install the app (recommended)

Open Chrome’s ⋮ menu and choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen” if shown.

4
Confirm the test alert

You should receive an Finlumiq test notification in your Android notification shade.

Development URL warning: 127.0.0.1 on your computer refers to that computer, not your phone. For phone testing, deploy the site to HTTPS. A plain LAN address such as http://192.168.x.x is not a secure origin for these features.
