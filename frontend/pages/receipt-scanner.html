💡 Tips for accurate scanning
Good lighting
Avoid shadows and glare over the printed text.
Keep it flat
Place the receipt on a plain, contrasting surface.
Capture all edges
Make sure the date and final amount are not cut off.
Verify the total
Always compare the extracted amount with the receipt before saving.
📷 Take a receipt photo
Allow camera access when your browser asks.
On phones, the rear camera is preferred. On laptops and desktops, the browser uses the available webcam. Camera access requires permission and a secure page (localhost or HTTPS).
